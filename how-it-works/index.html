Gate 01 · For strategy, analysis, ops, finance, research
The Quality Triangle.

Every analytical deliverable — a strategy memo, a competitive audit, a financial entry, a marketing recommendation — passes three checks before it ships:

Skeptic: Would a smart, hostile reviewer accept this? Are the claims defensible? Is the strongest counter-argument addressed?
Architect: Does this fit how your business actually runs — or is it a generic framework that won't survive contact with reality?
Security: Legal exposure · financial risk · brand harm · operational breakage · privacy violation — all screened.

Fails any pillar → it doesn't leave the firm. Gets logged, gets fixed, gets re-tested. The version you see has already survived three reviews you'll never see.

Gate 02 · For visual + creative deliverables
The Creative Board.

Every visual deliverable — a logo, a deck, a motion graphic, a social post, a website page, a sign proof — passes three different checks:

Vision: Does it carry the core idea it's meant to land? Or is it pretty without saying anything?
Craft: Typography, color, motion, layout, execution — all at the standard you'd expect from a senior in-house team, not an agency intern.
Brand-fit: Matches your brand's voice and design system — not Vector's instinct, not a stock template, yours.

Same discipline. Fails any pillar → doesn't ship. The version you approve has already survived three reviews.

The math on this: a senior partner at a consulting firm bills $800-$1,500 an hour and runs your work through their own review when they have ten minutes between meetings. PRAGMA's gates run on every deliverable, every time, with full discipline — at no marginal cost per check. The gate is the differentiator we'll never undercharge for.
