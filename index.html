05 — The architecture

Two layers.
One running system.

Every PRAGMA install ships two layers, both on your machine, both owned by you. The Operators — your reasoning bench. The Flock — your operations floor.

The Operators
Eight standard Operators — Keystone, Vector, Atlas, Lens, Scout, Pulse, Prism, Ledger — plus up to three custom roles built for your business.
Each owns one function. Strategy. Brand. Operations. Intelligence. Research. Owned presence. Alt-data. Finance.
Coordinated through a documented agent map. You engage them when judgment is required.
This is the reasoning bench. It works when you work with it.
The Flock
Twenty to thirty lightweight utility agents running as background processes on your machine. Always on. No session required.
Review monitor. Competitor watcher. GSC drift alert. Dashboard sync. Uptime check. They fire on schedule whether you're at your desk or not.
Your competitors' pricing changes at 2am. Your Flock catches it. Your inbox has the alert by morning.
This is the operations floor. It works while you sleep.

Other platforms run agents in their cloud. PRAGMA's run on yours — both layers, permanently, whether we're involved or not.
