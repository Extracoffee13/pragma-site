About

PRAGMA was built by an operator, not a consultant.

Before PRAGMA sold a single engagement, the system was running on a 40-year operator — Brand 9 Signs, a national signage business since 1986. Every agent in the kit comes from a shop that's been using it in production, not a whitepaper. That's the wedge.

Origin

PRAGMA was founded in 2026 — four decades of operating experience inside a single family of businesses, concentrated into an agent-operated firm. The lineage runs through Brand 9 Signs (founded 1986, today a national signage operator for homebuilders, government agencies, military bases, universities, and Fortune 500 campuses).

PRAGMA's operating model is built on a body of peer-reviewed research: Lakhani et al. (HBS, 2025) — The Cybernetic Teammate, Dell'Acqua et al. (HBS, 2023) — Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier, and the Frontier Firm model documented in Microsoft's Work Trend Index (2026) — translated into an installable operating system for small and mid-sized businesses.

Why PRAGMA exists

For thirty years, business consulting has had the same shape: a team flies in, observes for ninety days, hands the client a plan, and leaves. The client implements it alone — or doesn't. AI consulting today repeats the pattern with new vocabulary.

The founding thesis: that shape is broken under agentic AI. The Frontier Firm operating model — human-led, agent-operated, AI at the core of operations — is the structural alternative. The research has been published — by Lakhani and colleagues at Harvard Business School, by Microsoft's Work Trend Index, by Dell'Acqua, Mollick, and others. PRAGMA does the deployment.

The founding decision

PRAGMA doesn't ship architectures it hasn't run. The system was built on Brand 9 Signs — a 40-year signage operator — before PRAGMA sold a single engagement. The weekly proof-deck pipeline that used to take hours of designer work, across dozens of designers, runs in minutes now. DeckForge renders the client decks. Atlas is the real-time operations dashboard. Drones cover the install crews. The shop got smarter; the headcount didn't have to. The pricing reflects that. The retainer reflects that. The outcome share reflects that.

How PRAGMA relates to Brand 9 Signs

PRAGMA and Brand 9 Signs are separate legal entities with a shared operating foundation. They share the operating architecture (the Construct) and the agent design patterns. They do not share customers or contractual obligations — but the system was built on a real operator before it was sold to anyone else.

The shape of the firm

PRAGMA is ≤3 humans, by design. The agents are the labor; the architecture is the firm. There are no PRAGMA consultants. There are no PRAGMA principals. There are no associates being staffed on your engagement. The operating model PRAGMA installs on your business is the same operating model PRAGMA uses to serve you.

Why: hiring consultants to teach the Frontier Firm model would replicate the same Transformation Paradox Narayandas and Zhang (HBS, 2026) documented inside client orgs — the humans whose identity depends on the old model slow-roll the new one. PRAGMA built around that observation.

What we won't do

  • We don't advertise. Inbound and referral only in Year 1. Ads cheapen the institutional positioning.
  • We don't publish pricing. The application asks budget range. We respond with the structure that fits.
  • We don't take engagements that exceed the operator's capacity. That breaks the model.
  • We don't ship custom software. We design and deploy AI agent architectures. Software development is somebody else's expertise.
  • We don't hire consultants. The wedge requires it. The economics permit it.
