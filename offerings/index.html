How the work gets to you.

PRAGMA delivers in one of two engagement tracks. The work you receive is the same in either — the difference is in how it gets installed, configured, and handed off to your business.

SMB · Most clients
PRAGMA Install
We install the PRAGMA Operator OS on a dedicated machine for your business, configure it to your ecosystem (your tools, your calendar, your CRM, your owned-presence stack), and hand it off. Your team owns the OS after handoff — including the ability to extend it with new Operators as your business grows.

Three tiers depending on your complexity:
Install 1 · Starting at $7,500
Standard install
OS install on your dedicated machine. Five Operators activated. Standard configuration for common SMB tools (M365 / Google Workspace, Slack / Teams, QuickBooks / Square, GBP, LinkedIn, Facebook). 7–14 day delivery. Best for founder-led SMBs with a clean, common tool stack who want to begin running differently fast.
Install 2 · Custom, by discovery
Custom-configured install + 90-day monitor
Install 1, plus custom configuration of two or three Operators to your specific tools and workflows. 90-day post-install monitoring while your team adapts. 14–21 day delivery. Best for growing SMBs with operational complexity — multiple tools, multiple roles, multiple workstreams — that need the OS tuned to how the business actually runs.
Install 3 · Custom, by discovery
Custom + bespoke Operator development
Install 2, plus one to three fully custom Operators built bespoke for your vertical or unique workflows. 180-day monitoring. 30-day Sprint Build pattern with quarterly review. Best for SMBs whose business has truly unique operational shapes that the standard 7-Operator stack doesn't cover — and who want a documented operating moat by the end of the engagement.

A focused way in: Mirror Base.

Not every business needs all eight Operators on day one. Mirror Base is the owned-presence Operator — Pulse — installed on its own, as a complete product. It does everything a rented marketing platform does for a local business: your website, your local search, your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your social. Except you own all of it — on your domain, your hosting, your data, your machine. When you outgrow it, the other seven Operators bolt on. It's the front door to the OS.

Owned-presence Operator · standalone
What Mirror Base runs
A website on your own WordPress (we operate it; you own it). Google Business Profile and listings, maintained. A review engine that requests and answers in your voice. Brand-voice social tied to real completed jobs. Continuous SEO, AEO, and GEO on your actual site — not a locked template. Payments wired over your existing Stripe or Square, and a job-centric flow: lead → quote → job → review, on one screen. No new wallet, no vendor lock, nothing to learn.
the spear · closed-loop ranking repair

When your ranking slips or a lead goes missed, Mirror Base drafts the exact fix — a page rewrite, a schema injection, a Google post, a review response — you approve it in one tap, and it ships to your own site.

A rented platform can post and reply. It can't rewrite your site, because it doesn't own it at that depth. Mirror Base can — because it runs on your own install. That's the difference between software that markets at your business and an operator that runs inside it.

The difference that matters most comes the day you'd want to leave. When a rented platform disappears — or you cancel — your site, your reviews, and your domain history can go with it. Mirror Base is yours from day one. If PRAGMA vanished tomorrow, it keeps running. And if you're already on a rented platform, migration is part of the install: site, domain, Google profile, photos, and reviews come across intact. Switching isn't a teardown.
