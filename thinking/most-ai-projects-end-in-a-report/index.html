Most AI projects end in a report.
Ours end in a running system.

For thirty years, business consulting has had the same shape: a team flies in, observes for ninety days, hands you a plan, and leaves. AI consulting today repeats the pattern with new vocabulary. PRAGMA was built to be the operating-model alternative.

The shape of the problem

Steve Jobs said it best, paraphrased: consultants see a sliver. They observe one quarter of one part of your business, write up what they saw, recommend what to do about it, and leave. The work of actually changing how the business operates is yours, alone, after the deck closes.

The pattern survives because it's profitable for the firm doing the consulting. Five consultants billing $200/hour for ninety days produces $720,000 of revenue against a deliverable that imposes zero implementation risk. The client walks away with a binder. The firm walks away with a check. The business runs the same as before, except now there's a roadmap in a drawer.

AI consulting has imported this shape wholesale. An AI strategy assessment. A capabilities maturity model. A prioritized roadmap of use cases. A recommended pilot. A change-management plan. None of it actually does the work. The client gets a report; the work to deploy AI in the business still sits on the client's plate, exactly as it did before.

The deliverable has been a report for forty years. AI did not change the shape of consulting. The shape of consulting is now the constraint.

What the research actually says

Karim Lakhani and his co-authors at Harvard Business School ran a field experiment with 791 P&G professionals in 2025 — the Cybernetic Teammate study. The finding was unambiguous: individuals working with AI matched the performance of teams working without AI on breakthrough innovation tasks. The capacity multiplier from properly-deployed AI is large enough to substitute for entire human teams.

Lakhani et al. · HBS · 2025 · Cybernetic Teammate
"Individuals working with AI matched the performance of teams working without AI on breakthrough output. The augmented individual is not a smaller team — it is a different operating unit."

Microsoft's 2026 Work Trend Index documents the same shift at the firm level — they call it the Frontier Firm, human-led and agent-operated, with AI at the core of operations rather than at the edge. Dell'Acqua's earlier work on the Jagged Frontier (HBS, 2023, N=758) showed augmented workers exceeded baselines by 25-40% inside AI's capability set.

All three studies share a finding consulting reports systematically miss: the leverage is in the deployment, not the strategy. The strategy has been published. It's been published by HBS. It's been published by Microsoft. It's been published in HBR. It is not the bottleneck. The bottleneck is the operational work of building, installing, and running the agent architecture inside a real business that does not stop operating to accommodate the project.
