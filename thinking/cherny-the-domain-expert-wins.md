# The Domain Expert Wins

At Sequoia's AI Ascent 2026, Boris Cherny — the engineering lead on Claude Code at Anthropic — told a room of investors something that should change how every SMB owner, every PE acquirer, and every venture allocator thinks about the next decade:

> *"The best person to write accounting software, I think maybe even today, is not an engineer. It's a really good accountant. Because they know the domain really well, and coding is the easy part. It's knowing the domain that's the hard part."*
>
> *"And I think this is just obviously the future."*

This is not a prediction. It's a permission slip — issued by the engineer building the tool that makes it possible.

## Why this matters more than the AI takes you've read this year

Most "AI is the future" essays are written by AI vendors who want you to buy their AI. This one is different. Cherny is telling the audience at a top-tier venture conference that the binding constraint on AI deployment is NOT his team's product. It's not the model layer. It's not the engineering capacity. It's the **domain knowledge** that the model has to be wrapped in to do useful work.

The model layer is commoditizing. The vendor providing the model is on stage saying it.

The moat — the part that doesn't commoditize — is the operator who knows the business cold.

## What this means for the operator who's been running their business for 25 years

You don't need to hire engineers to bring AI into your business. You don't need to wait for the perfect SaaS tool. You don't need to attend an AI conference.

You need to take what you already know — the way the business actually runs, the customer patterns, the supplier rhythms, the seasonal pricing, the operational quirks that have kept you ahead of the textbook MBAs for two decades — and translate that knowledge into an agentic operating system that runs alongside you.

That is the PRAGMA Operator OS. It installs in 14-30 days. It runs on your hardware. It compounds your knowledge while you sleep.

## What this means for the PE acquirer

The acquisition multiple on an analog business compresses when the operator-buyer can deploy AI on top of the operator-seller's domain knowledge faster than a software-first competitor can hire the engineering to compete.

Hartley Capital's thesis, written in one sentence: *Buy the domain expert. Install the agentic OS. Compound the moat that doesn't commoditize.*

Cherny just said the same thing from the vendor side.

## What this means for the venture investor

Stop funding "the next foundation model." Stop funding "AI for X" wrappers that compete on access to the same model the buyer can rent for ten dollars a month. Fund the integration layer that productizes domain expertise. That's the only durable moat in a world where coding is the easy part.

That's what PRAGMA is.
