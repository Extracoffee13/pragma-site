Security & your data

The most-asked questions, ranked by how often we get them.

Where does my business data live during a PRAGMA engagement?
Your data lives on your own AI platform — whichever platform you already use, or the one we recommend during discovery based on your security posture, your team's workflow, and the AI capabilities you actually need. PRAGMA does not host your agents. PRAGMA does not host your data. We install the agents on your environment; we have the access we need to maintain them; the data stays with you.
Plain English: your stuff stays in your house. We come over to set up the tools and then leave them with you.
Do you train AI models on my data?
No. PRAGMA does not train any model on your data. Your prompts, your documents, your conversations, your business information — none of it is used to train any model. The agents PRAGMA installs run on whatever AI platform you're using; the data-use policies of that platform apply. During discovery we help you select a platform whose policies match your security needs.
Plain English: we don't feed your business into a machine that other people can later access.
Who at PRAGMA can see my data?
During setup, the people working on your engagement need access to design and test the agents — that's the principal (Robert) and any cleared collaborator on your specific engagement. Access is logged, scoped, and revocable. After Week 8, when the system is running on its own, PRAGMA's access drops to what's needed for monthly optimization (typically read-only on the agent configurations, not on the underlying client data).
Plain English: a small, named team during setup. Almost nothing after that.
Is my AI platform secure from attack?
Modern enterprise AI platforms (the ones we deploy on) carry SOC 2 Type II certifications, encryption at rest and in transit, single-sign-on integration, role-based access controls, and audit logging. The platform you choose during discovery — Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Workspace Gemini, or another — determines the specific security envelope. PRAGMA picks platforms by your security needs first, your team's familiarity second.
Plain English: the bank-grade AI platforms we use have the security a real business needs. We pick the right one for you.
What about HIPAA, PCI, SOC 2, GDPR — regulated industries?
If your business is regulated, PRAGMA matches your compliance regime. HIPAA-covered entities get HIPAA-compliant platforms (BAA-eligible enterprise AI tiers). PCI-scope businesses get architecture that keeps payment data out of agent reach. SOC 2-certified clients get the documentation their auditors need. GDPR-applicable operations get EU data-residency platforms and the records you need to demonstrate compliance. We flag any compliance constraint that changes the recommended architecture during discovery — before contract.
Plain English: if you have rules you have to follow, we follow them too. We'll tell you upfront which platform fits your rules.

What we need from you to begin

Three commitments. Everything else flows from these.

How much of my time will this take?
During the 30-day setup: roughly four hours per week of decision-maker time. Most of it concentrated in Weeks 1-2 (discovery) and Week 5 (deployment review). After Week 8 the system runs without daily involvement; the monthly retainer touchpoint is typically a 60-minute call.
Plain English: four hours a week for a month. About the time you'd spend on a single new hire's interview process.
What access will you need from me?
Read access to the systems we're integrating into the agents. Typically: your CRM, your accounting system, your collaboration tools (Slack/Teams/Notion), your email or calendar if relevant. Read-only on day one. Write access added selectively, only where the agent's job genuinely requires it, and only to specific scopes — never blanket admin. Every access grant is documented; every grant is revocable in one click.
Plain English: we'll ask for permission to read your tools. We don't take more than we need, and you can pull the plug any time.
What documents will I need to share?
Your existing operating documents — whatever you have. SOPs (if they exist), recent strategy materials, your brand voice samples, recent financial summaries, customer lists (anonymized if you prefer), the dashboards you currently use. If you don't have these documented, we build the documentation as part of the engagement — that's part of the work. You don't need to be "ready" to start. The engagement is partly about getting you ready.
Plain English: bring what you have. If you don't have something, we'll help you build it.
Do I need a technical team?
No. PRAGMA does the technical work. You don't need a "prompt engineer" on staff. You don't need to learn new tools — the agents are integrated into the tools you already use (Slack, email, Notion, your existing dashboards). The training PRAGMA delivers is on the operating discipline — how to work with the system — not on the underlying technology.
Plain English: you don't need to hire a tech person. We are the tech person.

The engagement mechanics

How long until I have a live system?
Thirty days from contract signature. Weeks 1-2: discovery and architecture. Weeks 3-4: agent specification and build. Weeks 5-6: deployment on your AI platform. Weeks 7-8: stabilization with daily PRAGMA touchpoints. After Week 8, the system runs without daily involvement; the monthly retainer takes over ongoing optimization.
Plain English: one month from when you sign. Live, not a roadmap.
What does PRAGMA cost?
The application asks budget range; PRAGMA responds with the structure that fits. Three tiers exist: pilot, standard, concierge. The structure is setup fee + monthly retainer + outcome share. The outcome share means when your business runs better, PRAGMA earns more — when it doesn't, we don't. Specific numbers communicated post-application because the right tier depends on the shape of your business, not a price list.
Plain English: three options. We figure out which one fits after you apply. Our pay rises and falls with your results.
Can I cancel the engagement?
Yes. The monthly retainer is month-to-month after the initial 30-day setup. Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. The agents you built stay with you — they're installed on your platform, not ours. PRAGMA's monthly optimization ends; your operating system continues. The initial setup fee is non-refundable once Week 1 work begins because the discovery is the work — the architecture we map in those first weeks is yours to keep even if you walk away.
Plain English: leave anytime after the first month. The agents stay with you regardless.
Who owns the work at the end?
You do. The agents, the data, the configurations, the documentation — all yours. PRAGMA does not retain ownership of the agent system, the prompts, the integrations, or the dashboards. The architecture is documented in your repository, on your platform, in your account. Client ownership is non-negotiable — it's not a feature, it's the operating principle.
Plain English: when we're done, all of it is yours. If you fire us tomorrow, you keep everything we built.
What happens if PRAGMA ceases operations?
Your operating system keeps running. Because PRAGMA installs agents on your AI platform — not a PRAGMA-hosted SaaS — there is no central server that can fail. The agents you depend on continue operating. You can hire someone else to maintain them, or take maintenance in-house using the documentation we ship at deployment. The architecture is platform-agnostic enough that any qualified engineering team can pick it up. No lock-in. Ever.
Plain English: even if PRAGMA disappears tomorrow, your business doesn't break.

Legal & paperwork

Do you sign NDAs?
Yes. PRAGMA signs mutual NDAs before any engagement discussion that involves your confidential information. A standard mutual NDA is available on request; we can also work with your firm's standard form. Discovery sessions, agent specifications, and any business data shared during the engagement are subject to the NDA. PRAGMA also signs Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) when GDPR or comparable regulations apply.
Plain English: yes, we sign confidentiality agreements before we talk about your business.
Who owns the intellectual property in the agents?
Anything built specifically for your business — your agent configurations, your custom prompts, your business-specific architecture — is owned by you. PRAGMA's pre-existing methodology (the Coca-Cola Test rubric, the agent map framework, the LISTEN/READ/WATCH output discipline) remains PRAGMA's. The engagement contract documents the IP boundary explicitly — nothing ambiguous about who owns what.
Plain English: anything we build for you is yours. The general method is ours. Boundaries written into the contract.
What about liability if something goes wrong?
The engagement contract includes a standard professional services liability framework — PRAGMA carries professional liability insurance, the contract caps damages at the fees paid for the relevant engagement period, and a mutual indemnification clause covers third-party claims arising from the work. Reasonable. Defensible. Negotiable for enterprise engagements with specific risk profiles.
Plain English: we carry insurance and our contract has the standard protections. If you have a lawyer, they won't blink at it.

While we're working together

What does Week 1 look like?
A 90-minute kickoff with you (the operator) and PRAGMA. We map how your business actually runs — not how it's organized on paper. Where your time goes. Which decisions consume your week. What three to five tasks eat hours and produce the lowest strategic value. From that map, we identify the agent set. By end of Week 1 you have a written discovery memo from PRAGMA that summarizes everything we heard and the proposed architecture.
Plain English: a long conversation about your business, then a written summary back to you.
What does Week 8 look like?
The system is live and producing real work. You're reviewing daily output (briefings, drafts, reports) from each Operator. PRAGMA is on call for tuning. By end of Week 8, you sign off that the system runs without our daily involvement. The relationship shifts to monthly retainer mode — one structured 60-minute touchpoint per month, plus on-demand support for material changes.
Plain English: by month-end you're running on it. We're available but not in your hair every day.
What's on the monthly retainer?
Three things. One: ongoing Self-Improving Loop maintenance — the protocol that lets the system learn from its own mistakes, processed monthly. Two: scheduled optimization — quarterly we re-evaluate each Operator's charter against the previous quarter's output, prune what's not working, sharpen what is. Three: change support — when your business changes (new revenue line, new geography, new team member), PRAGMA adapts the agents to match. All three covered by the retainer; no per-change fees.
Plain English: we keep the system smart, keep it tuned, and update it when your business changes — all included.
What if I want to add a new Operator later?
New Operators outside the original engagement scope are added as a separate engagement (sized by the work). The eight PRAGMA Operators — Keystone, Vector, Atlas, Lens, Scout, Pulse, Prism, Ledger — are the standard kit. A new custom Operator (a domain-specific role unique to your business) is typically a 30-60 day add-on engagement. The monthly retainer covers maintenance of the existing system; significant new builds are scoped separately.
Plain English: building something new is a new project. Maintaining what's there is on the retainer.
